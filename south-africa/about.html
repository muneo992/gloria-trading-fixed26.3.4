About Gloria Trading

Gloria Trading is a Japan-based used vehicle exporter, founded in 2006 and based in Natori, Miyagi Prefecture, Japan.

We source vehicles from Japanese auctions according to buyer requirements and arrange export from Japan. This South Africa site focuses on right-hand-drive EV and plug-in hybrid vehicles.

Gloria Trading does not currently list a South African office. Contact is with the Japan office.

Import requirements must be confirmed before shipment. Gloria Trading does not guarantee import approval or ride-hailing platform acceptance.
