What we can do

01

Hear your requirements

Send the model, year range, mileage, EV or PHEV preference, and any other details you already know. You do not need a complete specification.

02

Search Japanese auctions

We look for right-hand-drive vehicles on the Japanese auction market that match your request.

03

Share vehicle information

When a candidate is found, we share available photos, video, year, mileage, EV/PHEV type, battery and range information when known, and a quotation.

04

Quotation and export from Japan

We prepare a quotation from Japan. Purchase and export proceed only after you review the vehicle information and agree the terms.

Information we can provide

When a matching auction vehicle is found, Gloria Trading can typically share:

  • Model, year and mileage
  • Whether the vehicle is EV or PHEV
  • Battery information, when it is known
  • Driving range information, when it is known
  • Price / quotation
  • Photos
  • Video, when available

If a detail is not known, we leave it unknown. We do not invent battery condition, range or other specifications.

Import and intended use

Import requirements must be confirmed before shipment.

Gloria Trading does not guarantee a South African Import Permit, import approval, vehicle registration, or acceptance by Uber or another ride-hailing platform. Those checks sit with the buyer and the relevant authorities or platforms.

If you intend to use a vehicle for ride-hailing, say so in your request. We can look for suitable RHD EV or PHEV candidates. Platform eligibility must still be confirmed separately.
