How to Buy

  1. 1 — Tell us what you need

    Send the model or type, year range, mileage, EV or PHEV preference, and any other requirements you already know.

  2. 2 — We search Japanese auctions

    Gloria Trading looks for right-hand-drive vehicles that match your request. Availability depends on the current Japanese market.

  3. 3 — Review vehicle information and quotation

    When a candidate is found, review available photos, video, year, mileage, EV/PHEV type, battery and range information when known, and the quotation.

  4. 4 — You decide

    Receiving a candidate or quotation does not commit you to a purchase.

  5. 5 — Confirm import requirements, then purchase and export

    Import requirements must be confirmed before shipment. Gloria Trading does not guarantee a South African Import Permit, registration, or ride-hailing platform acceptance. Once you approve the vehicle and the purchase terms, we arrange purchase and export from Japan.

Gloria Trading is based in Japan and arranges export from Japan. Local import, duty, registration and platform eligibility in South Africa are the buyer’s responsibility to confirm.
