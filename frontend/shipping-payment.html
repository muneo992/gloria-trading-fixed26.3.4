Shipping route

RHD orders are primarily sourced in Japan and LHD orders in the UAE. Confirm the loading port, destination port, available shipping method and timing in your quote.

Payment

Review the proforma invoice for payment terms, currency and verified payment instructions before transferring funds.

FOB, CFR and CIF

Your quote specifies the trade term, named port and included costs. Ask which local port, clearance and inland costs apply.

Import eligibility

Check steering position, vehicle age, inspections and other local requirements with your importer or customs authority before ordering.
