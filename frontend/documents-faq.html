Documents & FAQ

What to check before requesting and confirming an RHD or LHD order.

Request RHD SourcingRequest LHD Sourcing

Can I request a particular model?

Yes. Tell us the Japanese brand, model, steering position, year range, mileage and budget you want.

What do the vehicle pages show?

RHD references are past transactions. LHD listings are sourcing candidates subject to availability and price reconfirmation.

Can I request an LHD vehicle if none are listed?

Yes. Send your requirements and we will check the current market for suitable candidates.

What documents are available?

Confirm the invoice, transport documents and any inspection or export documents required for your route in the quote. Auction sheets apply where available through auction sourcing.
